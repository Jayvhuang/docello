Frequently asked questions

What does cropping do?

It removes the same margin from all four edges of every page and shrinks the page to the cropped area.

Will my content be lost?

Only the outer margin you specify is removed. The central content stays intact.

Is it processed locally?

Yes. Cropping runs in your browser; the file is never uploaded.
