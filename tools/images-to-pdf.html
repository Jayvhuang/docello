Frequently asked questions

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WebP. WebP/GIF are converted automatically in your browser.

How is each image placed?

By default each image gets its own page sized to the image. You can also fit everything to A4 portrait.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Images are read locally and never leave your device.
