Frequently asked questions

Is merging PDFs safe on Docello?

Yes. Files are processed locally in your browser and never uploaded to any server.

Can I change the order of files before merging?

Yes. After adding files, use the up/down arrows in the list to set the exact merge order.

Are there file-size or page limits?

No. Docello is free with no file-size caps and no watermarks on the output.
