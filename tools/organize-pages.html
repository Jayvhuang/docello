Frequently asked questions

How do I delete a page?

List only the pages you want to keep, in order. Omitted pages are removed from the result.

Can I reorder pages?

Yes. Enter the page numbers in the new sequence, e.g. 3,1,2,4.

Will the output keep my content?

Yes. Pages are copied as-is; only their order changes.
