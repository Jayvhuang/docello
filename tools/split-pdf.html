Frequently asked questions

How do I extract certain pages?

Choose “Extract page ranges” and enter something like 1-3, 5, 7-9. Only those pages are saved into a new PDF.

What does “split every page” do?

It creates one PDF per page and bundles them into a single ZIP download.

Does splitting upload my file?

No. All splitting happens on your device in the browser.
